A new Home, a new repo list and a new board on iPhone.

• Home: what you captured today and this week, what needs a hand (abandoned tasks, unpublished ones, failing builds, unread reports, open pull requests, dormant repos), your capture rhythm, the latest captures, where work piles up, what GitHub knows about your repos, and the health of iCloud and GitHub sync
• Tasks: your whole backlog on one screen, by repo, with pending, recent, done and ideas, plus search
• Repos: dark, dense cards with the last push, the pending tasks and what only GitHub knows, fetched in a single request
• Board: one column at a time, with the columns as pills above and the microphone below
• Statistics: connect your app's backend from the board menu and see who uses it and what gets used, with its crashes and reports
• Voice: it no longer cuts you off or saves by itself. Review the card, edit it, keep dictating, then save. It knows your app names, and on iOS 26 with Apple Intelligence it shapes the title, details, kind and column on your device, even several tasks from one dictation
• Dictate from anywhere: a Home Screen and Lock Screen widget, Siri ("Add a task in RepoMind") and the Action Button open the app listening. Say the app and the task, like "In InSync, fix the login"
• Agent: each task carries its kind as a GitHub label and can be handed to a coding agent
• Notifications: tapping a report or a new issue opens the task or the report itself, they read cleaner and stack by app, and they show up even with the app open
• App Store: which version of each of your apps is published, which one is in review or rejected, the latest TestFlight build and a warning when it was never sent, and your downloads per day. Connect it in Settings
• Pro: alerts for failing builds, new releases, security alerts and crashes, and a panel with all your apps
• Profile under Settings, search at the bottom next to the tabs

iPad and Mac keep the classic layout for now.
