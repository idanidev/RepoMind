What's new:

• An onboarding on first launch, showing a real preview of each feature
• Folders to group your projects: create, rename and move repos between them. Deleting a folder never deletes the projects inside it
• A new "GitHub Issues Sync" setting to see at a glance which repositories publish their tasks, and turn it on one by one or everywhere at once

Fixes:

• When your GitHub token expires you can now enter a new one without signing out, keeping your projects and tasks
• Recovered tasks that had lost their column and were showing on no board at all
• Tasks that fail to reach GitHub now say why, and can be retried from "Today"
• iCloud sync problems name the actual cause instead of a generic error
• The keyboard opens on its own again when creating a task
• Opening a task shows the delete button, and deleting now asks for confirmation
