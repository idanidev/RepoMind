A new home and a new board on iPhone.

• The home is now a dark, dense list of cards: pending tasks, unread reports, tasks not yet published as issues, open pull requests, the latest release and a failing build — each only when there is something to say
• Folders are sections, favorites come first, and a long press on a repo offers favorite, folder, icon, archive and delete
• The board shows one column at a time, with the columns as pills above and the add bar and the microphone below
• Pull down to refresh: your repos, the tasks your agent closed on GitHub, and what GitHub knows about each repo, fetched in a single request that leaves your rate limit alone
• A Profile tab with your accounts, plan and purchases

iPad and Mac keep the classic layout for now.
