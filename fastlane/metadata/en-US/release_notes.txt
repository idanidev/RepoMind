Fixes:

• Projects no longer appear duplicated. The cause was a GitHub account registered twice, which made every sync insert fresh copies of all your repositories
• Icons no longer vanish on refresh. They were never lost: what you saw were those new copies, which never had an icon in the first place
• Opening a project no longer means waiting to see what your agent closed

New:

• An ideas column: pick it in each project's settings and its tasks stop being published as issues and stop counting as pending. A half-formed idea is not a task
• A notification when an issue opened outside the app arrives, grouped by repository
• On Mac, configuring a project's icon is now in the context menu — with no swipe gesture, it was the one action you could not reach
