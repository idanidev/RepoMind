DICTATE, DON'T TYPE
Hold the mic and talk. RepoMind transcribes it and files the task in whichever project you choose. It listens in two languages at once, so you never have to switch gears mid-sentence. Attach a screenshot and the context travels with the task.

YOUR CODING AGENT READS THEM
This is the part no other app does. Install RepoMind Bridge and Claude Code, Cursor or Codex pick up your tasks when they open the repository — marking them in progress, asking you when something is ambiguous, closing them when the work is done. You dictate from the sofa; by the time you sit down, it has already started.

YOUR TASKS ARE GITHUB ISSUES
Every task is published as an issue in its repository, with its column as a label. Moving a card to Done closes the issue. And it works both ways: whatever gets closed on GitHub shows up as done on the board, and issues opened outside the app come in on their own.

ONE BOARD PER PROJECT
Each repository gets its own columns, with a separate one for ideas so a half-formed thought never ends up published as an issue. Folders to group projects. And a Today screen that gathers whatever needs attention across all of them.

ICLOUD SYNC
iPhone, iPad and Mac, always the same. No accounts and no servers of ours: your data travels through your own private iCloud. And when something fails, the app tells you exactly what, instead of going quiet.

FREE AND PRO
Free: 3 repositories, 1 GitHub account, 3 columns per board. Pro: everything unlimited, one single payment. No subscriptions.
